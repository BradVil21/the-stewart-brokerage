A note from our founder

Why this brokerage is small business only

Most brokerages chase the hundred-person groups and hand everyone else to a service queue. We made the opposite choice.

If you run a business with three, fifteen, or sixty people on payroll, you have felt it: the renewal letter nobody explains, the premium jump nobody warned you about, the "your call is important to us" hold music when a claim goes wrong. Not because anyone is malicious — because at a big firm, a small group is a rounding error.

Here, the small group is the entire practice. That focus is why we already know which carriers say yes to a six-person roster, which networks actually hold up in your county, and what a fair renewal looks like before your paperwork ever goes out.

"You are not getting handed to a call centre. You get one licensed broker who knows your renewal date, your census, and your budget — and answers when it matters."

— Petrina Stewart, Principal Broker
