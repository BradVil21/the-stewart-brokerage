About Petrina

Who I work with, and how

I place group health coverage for small businesses — the three-person shop, the fifteen-person restaurant, the sixty-person contractor. That is the whole practice.

Most owners come to me at one of two moments: a renewal letter arrived with a number nobody explained, or they are offering coverage for the first time and do not know where to start. Both are solvable, and both start the same way — with an honest look at your census, your budget, and what your team actually needs from a plan.

What I do day to day is unglamorous and it matters: build the census, run it across the carriers writing your state, model contribution levels so you can see the real budget impact before you commit, check that the network covers the doctors your people already use, run the enrollment, and then handle the new hires, the terminations, the ID cards and the claim that got processed wrong.

You get one licensed broker who knows your renewal date and answers the phone. Not a service queue.

Credentials

How to verify me

Producer licences are public record. You should check anyone you are about to hand your company’s benefits to — including me.

  • Licensed insurance producer — licence #3000270243. Look it up in the Florida Department of Financial Services licensee search, or the National Insurance Producer Registry, and you will find the same name that appears on your policy paperwork.
  • Practice scope — small group health only, 1 to 100 employees. No individual business, no large group.
  • States served — Florida, Texas and Nevada.
  • Cost to you — none. Broker compensation is built into the premium and is identical whether you enrol through a broker or go direct to the carrier.
