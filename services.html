Our services

Coverage designed around your team

Four core services, one point of contact, and a plan that keeps working long after the enrollment paperwork is signed.

01 — Coverage

Nationwide PPO Plans

Flexible, high-quality coverage that follows your people across the country.

Remote teams, travelling crews, and multi-state contractors get punished by narrow HMO networks. We source true nationwide PPO group plans so an employee in Denver has the same access as one in Fort Lauderdale — same plan, same card, same renewal date.

  • 1
    One plan across every state you operate in

    No more juggling separate policies for each office or each contractor's home state.

  • 2
    Real provider choice

    Your team keeps the doctors they already see instead of restarting care with an in-network stranger.

  • 3
    $0 and low-deductible tiers

    We model several deductible levels so you can see the tradeoff between premium and out-of-pocket exposure.

02 — Strategy

Tailored Group Plan Design

Custom benefits built around your business — not a template pulled off a shelf.

Plan design is where the money is made or lost. We start from your census, your contribution budget, and how your team actually uses care, then build the structure around it. Mixed W-2 and 1099 rosters, seasonal headcount swings, and owner-heavy groups are all normal here.

  • 1
    Contribution modelling

    See exactly what 50%, 75%, or 100% employer contribution does to your monthly cost before you commit.

  • 2
    Tiered options

    Offer a base plan and a buy-up so employees choose their own level instead of everyone paying for the richest tier.

  • 3
    Renewal strategy, not renewal surprise

    We start the renewal conversation 90 days out, with market comparisons in hand.

03 — Access

Telehealth & Modern Wellness

Care your team will actually use, on the device already in their pocket.

A benefit nobody uses isn't a benefit. Most of our options include virtual primary care, behavioral health, and 24/7 urgent-care consults at little or no cost per visit — which keeps minor issues out of the emergency room and off your claims history.

  • 1
    24/7 virtual urgent care

    Typically $0 copay, no appointment, no half-day off the job site.

  • 2
    Mental health & counselling

    Virtual sessions with licensed clinicians, included on many plans.

  • 3
    Prescription savings programs

    Discount networks that often beat the plan's own pharmacy copay on common generics.

04 — Protection

Supplemental & Voluntary Benefits

The coverage that fills the gaps medical alone leaves open.

Dental, vision, life, accident, critical illness, and short-term disability — offered as employer-paid or voluntary employee-paid, and administered alongside your medical plan so there's one enrollment, one renewal, and one person to call.

  • 1
    Dental & vision

    The two most-requested add-ons, and usually the cheapest way to make a package feel complete.

  • 2
    Accident & critical illness

    Cash benefits paid directly to the employee — valuable for physical trades with high deductibles.

  • 3
    Group life & disability

    Guaranteed-issue amounts with no medical questions for most group sizes.

What's included with every engagement

Service doesn't stop at enrollment

  • Employee education

    Live or recorded sessions so your team understands what they're signing up for — in plain language.

  • Onboarding & terminations

    We handle adds and drops so your admin isn't chasing carrier portals.

  • Claims advocacy

    When a claim is denied or a bill looks wrong, we get on the phone with the carrier — not you.

  • Annual market review

    Every year we re-shop the market to confirm you're still in the right place.
